# spiral of progress in quality
A simple definition of quality is "fitness for use". That definition
must quickly be enlarged, because there are many uses and users.

A convenient way to.show some of the many uses and users is
through the "spiral of progress in quality" (Figure 1—1). We shall
refer to that simply as The Spiral.
